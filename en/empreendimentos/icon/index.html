Discover Icon

190 to 637 m² · 4 suites

Icon is the first development in the Base Genius Loci collection, the highest category of projects by Base Incorporações. It is built on one idea: architecture should start from the spirit of the place. In Brasília, that means celebrating the sky, the greenery and the open space. The façade is biophilic, with vegetation integrated into the building, and the pilotis free up the ground floor.

Just 40 four-suite apartments across 6 floors plus a rooftop level, open to natural light and to the unobstructed views only Brasília offers. Generous floor plans, finishes above market standard and a gym fully equipped by Technogym, minutes away from the seat of government and connected to the Monumental Axis.
