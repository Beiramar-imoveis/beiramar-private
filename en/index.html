About Private

The luxury division of Beiramar.

Beiramar Private curates exclusive homes in Brasília’s most valued neighborhoods, inside the Plano Piloto, the city’s modernist core and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. We focus on location, architecture, construction standards and appreciation potential.

Our service is advisory, discreet and personal, led by specialists who understand the timing, profile and expectations of those who want to live or invest safely in the luxury market.
